## Advisories carried OPEN (from the panel, not closed by the fixers)

**1. The tutor asymmetry (HIGH — the headline; PARKED WITH THE DEVELOPER).** The builder's
claims got a deterministic re-runnable checker plus a Steward fabrication probe. The tutor's
claims — the ones deciding what a human believes he understands — got prose and tests that
grep the prose. Three cheats, none detectable: (1) the educator writes the question, the
rubric, judges demonstrated-vs-miss, records the row AND clears the registry gate — the
generator is the sole evaluator of the education gate, and Principle #3 is never applied
here; (2) the only instrument reading the result is `AVG(score)` in `/retro`/`/meta-review`,
which reads better the more the tutor declares everything demonstrated first ask — the
measurement's gradient points at lying; (3) the anti-synonym-swap rule is enforced by a
self-declared label in the reteach event. The captured `learner`/`evidence` answers, the only
evidence that could refute any of this, are read by nothing. The developer's standing brief
("DO NOT build an independent grader — the grade was never the point") does not settle this,
because the finding is about the gate marking ITSELF complete, not about grading. **Developer
decision required; do not build on this surface until it is rendered.**

**2. The ratchet does not propagate; only the burden does (HIGH).**
`scripts/lineage/manifest.py::FRAMEWORK_PATHS` = `['.claude/', 'scripts/', 'CLAUDE.md',
'docs/templates/', 'docs/adr/']` — `tests/` is excluded. Four derived projects receive the

`tests/test_education_gate.py` or `tests/test_paths_not_taken.py`. Downstream, the Bloom
ratio can re-invert exactly as it did on 2026-05-12 and nothing will fail. Fix requires
developer sign-off (add the files to `FRAMEWORK_PATHS`, or move the assertions into a
`scripts/` module). Same standing limitation as `tests/test_command_sql.py`.
