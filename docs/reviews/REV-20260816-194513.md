## The meta-finding

> **"The boundary tracked what the tooling made LOUD."**

Every **out-of-range** citation (`#8`/`#9`) was fixed. Every **concept-mismatch** citation
(in-range, wrong principle) was not. Scope was drawn by the detector rather than by the change's
actual blast radius — *"exactly what Principle #3 predicts"* of a single-context author.

**The clean tell — a contradiction inside the change's own reasoning:** `FRAMEWORK.md` was excused
as *"not in FRAMEWORK_PATHS so it does not propagate"* while `tests/` was deferred **because**
*"the public promotion is a git push of the whole tree."* Both arguments live in artifacts this
change touched. Only one can be true. Under the correct (whole-tree) model, `FRAMEWORK.md` ships
publicly on this PR. No single-context author reliably catches that.

### B3 — The change's own completeness claim is not auditable
